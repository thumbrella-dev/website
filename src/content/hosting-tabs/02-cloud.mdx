Thumbrella provides an online service for free. Sign up with your existing
Github or Google account with two clicks. No payment info or additional data
required.

- All media formats and features supported
- Caching CDN to over 300 regions
- The primary use cases are for free accounts

Once registered you will receive an authentication token, which also acts as
your connection string. Set your `$TBR_CONNECT` environment variable to this
token and use any of the available client libraries.
